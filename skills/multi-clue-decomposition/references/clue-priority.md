# Clue Priority Quick Reference

Use this ranking to decide which clues to put into your first `search` query:

| Priority | Clue Type | Example Conversion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (Best) | Named award, molecule, invention, treaty, rare event | "regulatory molecule" + "cancer" -> `plant hormone regulatory molecule` |
| 2 | Exact year, count, measurement, or competition | "same year as bachelor's degree" + "competition" -> `Olympics 1932 scientist` |
| 3 | Institution / city / specific military or organizational link | "served in army on continent of origin" -> `Swedish army` / `Caltech PhD` |
| 4 (Verify only) | Broad era, zodiac sign, personality traits | Use `find_in_doc` to confirm birth month/year AFTER finding a candidate |

Rule of thumb: 2 concrete clues in a 5-word query outperform 6 paraphrased clues in a 40-word query.
